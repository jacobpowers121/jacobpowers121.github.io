Privacy Policy

Sort Shop · Effective October 26, 2026

The short version

Sort Shop does not collect, share or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

Sort Shop is made by Powercore Game Studio (“we”). You can reach us at powercore.game.studio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The game doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and it doesn't send information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (levels completed, stars, gems, Main Street, sound and vibration preferences) are saved on your phone so you can pick up where you left off. If your phone's backup is turned on (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your phone may include this game data in its backups. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we can't see them.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share on a Daily Shelf result, your phone's share sheet opens with a short text summary. You choose where it goes. We don't see what you share.

Advertising and purchases

This version of Sort Shop shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. If a future version adds ads, analytics or purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released and describe exactly what is collected and why.

Children

Sort Shop isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because we don't collect personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all game data at any time by deleting the app (and, if you want, its data in your phone's backups).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email powercore.game.studio@gmail.com.
