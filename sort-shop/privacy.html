Privacy Policy

Sort Shop · Effective October 26, 2026

The short version

Sort Shop doesn't ask for or collect your personal information itself, and there are no accounts. Your game progress stays on your phone. Sort Shop is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad software collects some device and usage data to show and measure those ads. On iPhone you choose whether to allow tracking, and in Europe you choose whether to allow personalized ads.

Who we are

Sort Shop is made by Powercore Game Studio (“we”). You can reach us at powercore.game.studio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and the game doesn't send information about you to us. We only see totals reported by Google, such as how many ads were shown.

Advertising

Sort Shop is free because it shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a hint or an extra shelf. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app collects:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking) and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location, based on your IP address
  • Usage and ad data, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance data from the ad software

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We ask Google to keep ads suitable for a general audience.

Tracking on iPhone: Sort Shop asks your permission before allowing tracking. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you still see ads, but they aren't personalized with the advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Europe, the UK and Switzerland: you're asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choices any time in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (levels completed, stars, gems, Main Street, sound and vibration preferences) are saved on your phone so you can pick up where you left off. If your phone's backup is turned on (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your phone may include this game data in its backups. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we can't see them.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share on a Daily Shelf result, your phone's share sheet opens with a short text summary. You choose where it goes. We don't see what you share.

Children

Sort Shop isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Allow or deny tracking on iPhone in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Change your ad consent in the game under Settings › Privacy choices (Europe, the UK and Switzerland).
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your phone's settings.
  • Remove all game data by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email powercore.game.studio@gmail.com.
